I hold a degree in Information Systems from the University Center of Jales (2007) and a master's degree in Electrical Engineering from the Universidade Estadual Paulista Júlio de Mesquita Filho (2009). I am currently Adjunct Professor I at the Federal University of Tocantins. He has experience in Electrical Engineering, with emphasis on Electronic Circuits. I am currently a PhD student at the Institute of Computing, UNICAMP, researching approximate accelerators in FPGA.
